Word Lantern Help

Privacy policy

Your data stays with you

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Word Lantern is a word puzzle game for Android and the web, made by Ramlakhan Madheshiya (theramlakhan.com). In this policy, "we" means the developer.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases, and it does not send any data over the network while you play.

What is stored on your device

Your progress (cleared levels, coins, streaks, achievements, words found in an unfinished level) and settings (music, sounds, vibration) are saved locally: in the app's private storage on Android, or in your browser's storage for this site on the web. You can erase it at any time with Settings > Reset progress, by clearing the app's data, or by clearing this site's data in your browser. Uninstalling the app removes it.

Website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages behind Cloudflare, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) to deliver and protect the site. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. We do not use these logs to identify you.

Permissions

On Android the game asks only for vibration, used for short haptic feedback that you can turn off in Settings.

Children

Word Lantern is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone.
