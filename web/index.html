How it plays

One grid. Every tile is part of a word.

W

Trace to find

Drag up, down, left or right through the letters. Each theme word fits in exactly one place, so a correct word is never "in the wrong spot".

✦

Shadow tiles

From chapter 4 on, some tiles are dark. Finding a word next to them spreads the light, so the order you solve in matters.

B

Beacons

From chapter 5 you meet beacons: find their word and beams light up the whole row and column at once.

8

8 chapters, 8 ideas

Start with the words written out, then words that bend, hidden words, shadows, beacons, two themes and a mystery theme. 320 levels, each chapter climbing from easy to an extreme level 40.

★

Three stars

No wrong words and no hints earns three stars. Every chapter also has its own achievement and a coin bonus, and there is a fresh daily puzzle with a streak bonus.

✓

Fair and calm

Every level is checked by a solver. Hints are optional, your first Spark each day is free, and out of coins you can watch a short video instead. No pop-up ads, ever.
