How it plays

One grid. Every tile is part of a word.

W

Trace to find

Drag up, down, left or right through the letters. Each theme word fits in exactly one place, so a correct word is never "in the wrong spot".

✦

Shadow tiles

From the Harbor on, some tiles are dark. Finding a word next to them spreads the light, so the order you solve in matters.

B

Beacons

In the Canyon you meet beacons: find their word and beams light up the whole row and column at once.

8

8 worlds, 160 levels

From 3×3 meadows to 7×8 starfields, each world adds size, longer words and deeper shadows. 46 hand-picked themes.

D

Daily lantern

A fresh puzzle every day, with a growing coin bonus for keeping your streak alive.

✓

Fair and calm

Every level is checked by a solver. Hints are optional, your first Spark each day is free, and there are no ads at all.
