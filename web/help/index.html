Playing Word Lantern

The basics

Each level is a grid of letters on a theme, shown at the top (for example Fruit or Weather). Every tile belongs to exactly one hidden word. Drag through neighbouring tiles (up, down, left or right, never diagonally) to spell a word, then let go. Correct words light up. Light every tile to clear the level.

The boxes above the grid show how many words there are and how long each one is. Words read in the direction you trace them. If you slide back onto the previous tile, the last letter is removed.

Shadows and beacons

Shadow tiles (from the Harbor) hide their letters. Find any word touching a shadow and it lights up. Beacons (from the Canyon) carry a small flame; when their word is found, beams light every tile in that row and column.

Hints and coins

  • Spark (25 coins) reveals the next letter of a word on the grid and above it. Your first Spark each day is free.
  • Lantern (60 coins) finds a whole word for you.
  • Earn coins by clearing a level for the first time (+10, +5 more without hints), finishing a world (+50), the daily lantern (+30, more with a streak) and achievements.
  • Rewards are paid once: replaying a cleared level is just for fun.

Worlds and the daily lantern

Levels open one at a time. Clear 15 levels in a world to open the next. The daily lantern changes at local midnight; finishing it on consecutive days builds a streak.

Saving

Progress saves automatically on your device, including words found in a level you leave half way. In the browser, progress is stored by your browser for this site; clearing site data erases it. Nothing is uploaded.

Controls

Touch or mouse: drag to trace. Keyboard: H for a Spark, Esc for pause and back. On Android, the Back button works everywhere.
